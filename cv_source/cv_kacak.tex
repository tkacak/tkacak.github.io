% ================= RESEARCH PROFILE =================
\cvsection{Research Profile}
My work centers on how many factors are really there in a scale, and how that decision holds up under ordinal and mixed-format data, cross-loadings, hierarchical and bifactor structures, and departures from standard assumptions --- mostly through large Monte Carlo simulation studies. I extend this into mixture modeling, using finite mixture, latent class, and factor mixture models to uncover unobserved respondent subgroups (careless or disengaged responders, distinct response styles) and to test whether measurement properties hold across them. Alongside the factor-analytic tradition, I use psychometric network models as a complementary lens on construct structure. A fourth line applies these questions to large-scale assessment, studying measurement invariance and item-format effects using PISA, PIRLS, TIMSS, and ICILS data.
